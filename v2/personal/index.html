Business owners

Own a business too?

Many of our personal clients also own a business or hold shares in a corporation. Your personal and corporate taxes affect each other, from how you pay yourself to how you plan for retirement or succession.

We can look after both sides together. The same local team prepares your personal return and your corporate tax and year-end financial statements, so your planning stays consistent and you only have to explain your situation once.
