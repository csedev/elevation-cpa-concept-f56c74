Personal tax

Personal tax returns and planning in Durham Region

Elevation CPA prepares personal tax returns and plans ahead with individuals and families from our offices in Port Perry, Uxbridge and Whitby.

Book a personal tax appointment Download the tax checklist

Our approach

More than filling in forms

Whether you are a contractor or a small business owner, there is seldom such a thing as a simple tax return. We look at your income streams, your age and the other parts of your situation that affect your tax. The goal is to reduce or defer the tax you pay this year, which means arranging things legally so it’s paid later, and to help you plan for the years ahead.

Tax rules change every year. We keep up with those changes so you don’t miss a deduction or credit you’re entitled to. If something new applies to you, we’ll explain it in plain language and tell you what to do next.

Who we help

Who we prepare returns for

We prepare personal returns for people in many different situations. Whether you earn a salary, run a business, collect rent or take dividends from your corporation, we’ll prepare your return accurately and explain what it means for you.

  • Individuals and families

    Employment income, pensions, investment income, credits and deductions, prepared accurately and on time for you and your household.

  • Self-employed and contractors

    Business income and expenses, including vehicle and home office costs, reported correctly so you claim what you’re entitled to.

  • Rental property owners

    Rental income and expenses reported properly, with advice on what you can claim and the records you should keep.

  • Business owners and partners

    Personal returns that line up with your business or partnership, so income is reported correctly on both sides.

  • Shareholders taking salary or dividends

    Personal returns for owners who pay themselves from their corporation. We’ll explain how salary and dividends are taxed and plan the mix with you.

  • Planning for owner-managers and directors

    Personal tax planning and financial planning for owner-managers, directors and executives, including retirement planning and reviewing the tax impact of your investments with your advisor.

If you have US income, US property or US citizenship, see our cross-border and US tax services.

Getting started

How filing with us works

Getting started is simple. Here is what to expect.

    Collect your tax slips, receipts and any letters from the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA). Use the extra checklists if you have rental, self-employment or other income.

    We file your return with the CRA and explain your refund or balance owing, plus anything to plan for next year.

Deadlines

Key personal tax deadlines

Most personal tax returns are due April 30.

If you or your spouse or common-law partner are self-employed, your filing deadline is later. Any balance owing is still due April 30, so it’s worth getting your documents to us early.

During tax season our offices keep longer hours. Appointments outside regular hours can also be arranged.
