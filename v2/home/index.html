Three practices, one name

Elevation in Port Perry, Mad Accountant in Uxbridge and Copetti and Co in Whitby are now one firm: Elevation CPA. The Whitby office will take the Elevation name in January 2027.

If you’re an existing client, nothing about your service changes. You keep the same local office and the same team you already know.

Our approach

How we work

Timeliness, accessibility and understanding guide everything we do. Here is what that means in practice.

  1. 01

    Work done on time

    We plan around your filing deadlines and year-end so nothing is left to the last minute.

  2. 02

    Easy to reach

    Meet us at the office or by video call. We also book appointments outside regular office hours.

  3. 03

    Taxes you understand

    We’ll explain your return, your financial statements and your options in plain language, so you can plan ahead with confidence.

  4. 04

    Clear fees

    We discuss fees with you before work begins, so you know what to expect.
