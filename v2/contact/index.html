Before you get in touch

Common questions

View client resources

What should I bring to a first meeting?

Bring anything that helps us understand your situation, such as your most recent tax return or financial statements. The personal tax checklists on our Client Resources page list what we usually need.

Do you publish your fees?

No. Fees depend on your needs, so we discuss them with you at the consultation, before any work begins.

How do I send documents securely?

Please don’t send documents through the contact form. Our Client Resources page explains how to share files with us securely.

I was a Copetti and Co or Mad Accountant client. Who do I contact?

Mad Accountant in Uxbridge and Copetti and Co in Whitby are now part of Elevation CPA. The Whitby office becomes Elevation in January 2027. Contact the same office you’ve always used. You’ll find the phone numbers and addresses on our Locations page.
