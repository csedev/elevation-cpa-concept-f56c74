Not sure where to start?

Which service do I need?

Find the situation that sounds like yours. If none fit, or more than one does, book a consultation and we’ll explain your options.

  • I’m starting a business

    We’ll help you choose the right structure, such as sole proprietorship or corporation, and register with the federal and provincial governments.

    Start with Business advisory

  • I’m applying for a loan or financing

    Lenders usually ask for up-to-date financial statements. We prepare them and explain what they show.

    Start with Corporate tax and financial statements

  • I’m falling behind on remittances

    Late payroll or HST remittances, the amounts you collect and pass on to the CRA, can lead to penalties and interest. We’ll help you catch up and stay current.

    Start with Bookkeeping, payroll and HST

  • I received a letter from the CRA

    Don’t ignore it. We’ll review the letter, explain what the CRA is asking for and respond on your behalf where needed.

    Start with CRA disputes and audit support

  • I’m selling my business

    Plan ahead. We help with valuation, cash flow analysis, tax planning and the transition to new owners.

    Start with Business advisory

  • My business earns US income

    You may need to file US returns even if your US activity is limited. We’ll tell you what to file and when.

    Start with Cross-border and US tax
