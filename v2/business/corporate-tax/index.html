Corporate tax and year-end financial statements

We prepare your year-end financial statements and your corporate tax return together, and we plan your corporate tax around your goals. For owner-managed corporations across Durham Region, from our offices in Port Perry, Uxbridge and Whitby.

Why it matters

Why your financial statements matter

Financial statements show where your business stands today and how it has changed over time. They are the documents a bank asks for when you apply for a loan or financing, and the documents a buyer asks for if you sell your business. Keeping them up to date means you are ready when those moments come.

They also help you run the business. If you don’t know what you spend in each category, it is hard to decide where to cut, where to invest or how to price. We’ll explain what your statements are telling you, line by line, so you can plan ahead with confidence.

Our services

What we do for your corporation

Owner-managed corporations need tax planning, not only compliance. Here is what we handle for you.

  • Year-end financial statements

    We prepare your annual financial statements as a compilation, with comparative figures from the prior year so you can see trends in your margins and costs. These statements support your tax filing and your conversations with lenders.

  • Monthly or quarterly statements

    If you want a closer view of your numbers during the year, we can prepare statements monthly or quarterly instead of only at year-end. You choose the timing that suits your business.

  • Corporate tax returns

    We prepare and file your corporation’s annual tax return alongside your year-end statements, so the numbers match and nothing is done twice.

  • Corporate tax planning

    We look at how you take money out of your corporation, including the mix of salary and dividends, and plan with your personal and business goals in mind. We’ll explain the trade-offs in plain language.

  • Reorganizations and structure planning

    When your business changes, its structure may need to change too. We help plan reorganizations and corporate structures with the tax effects considered before decisions are made.

  • Compliance review before filing

    Before anything is filed, we review your expenses, deductions and year-end adjustments to make sure they are valid, correct and in line with your plan.

The process

How year-end works

  1. 01

    We confirm your year-end and deadlines

    We confirm your fiscal year-end and the filing and payment deadlines that apply to your corporation, so you know what is due and when.

  2. 02

    You send us your books or records

    Send your bookkeeping file or records to us through our secure upload. If your books need work first, our bookkeeping team can help.

  3. 03

    We review, adjust and discuss planning

    We review your records, make the year-end adjustments needed and talk with you about planning options, such as how you pay yourself.

  4. 04

    We prepare your statements and return

    We prepare your year-end financial statements and your corporate tax return together.

  5. 05

    We walk you through the results

    We go over the results with you, explain what they mean and set out a plan for next year.
