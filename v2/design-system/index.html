Plain answers

Questions we hear often

What are year-end financial statements?

They’re the yearly summary of your business: what it earned and spent (the income statement) and what it owns and owes (the balance sheet). Your corporation needs them to file its tax return, and banks often ask for them too.

What does it mean to defer tax?

Deferring tax means arranging things legally so tax is paid later rather than now, for example by leaving profit in your corporation until you need it. It isn’t avoiding tax, but paying later often means paying at a lower rate, and the money works for you in the meantime.

Can we meet by video instead of coming in?

Yes. Any meeting can be by video, and you can send documents through our secure upload instead of dropping them off.

I was a Mad Accountant or Copetti and Co client. What changes?

The name on your invoices and emails. Your Uxbridge or Whitby office stays open, and your file stays with us.
