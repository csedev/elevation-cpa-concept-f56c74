About Elevation CPA

One local CPA firm, three Durham offices

Elevation CPA is a CPA-led firm serving businesses and families from offices in Port Perry, Uxbridge and Whitby. We handle personal and corporate tax, bookkeeping, estate planning and audit, with one team and one standard of service across all three offices.

How we work

Clear advice, on time, with no surprises

Timeliness, accessibility and understanding are the core of how we work. We focus on two things: tax planning and client service.

Book a consultation

  • Plain-language advice

    We’ll explain your finances in plain language, so you can take control of your personal and corporate finances and plan ahead for tax and estate matters. If a term is unfamiliar, we’ll explain it.

  • Fees confirmed up front

    You’ll know what to expect before we start, including what the work will cost. We confirm fees up front, so there are no hidden fees on your bill.

  • Meetings that suit you

    Our offices are open Monday to Friday, and we’re available by appointment outside those hours. We also offer video meetings for new and existing clients, booked to suit your schedule.
