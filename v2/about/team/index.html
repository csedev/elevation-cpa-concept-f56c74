Credentials

What the letters after our names mean

When you see these letters below, you know who is qualified to handle each type of work on your file.

See our audit and review services
CPAChartered Professional Accountant

CPA stands for Chartered Professional Accountant. It is Canada’s professional accounting designation. To earn it, an accountant completes university-level education, a professional program, exams and supervised practical experience.

CPAs in Ontario are regulated by CPA Ontario and must follow its code of conduct and keep up with ongoing professional development.

LPALicensed Public Accountant

LPA stands for Licensed Public Accountant. This is a separate licence from CPA Ontario that allows an accountant to sign audit and review engagements.

If your lender, investors or a funding body ask for audited or reviewed financial statements, the engagement must be led by a licensed public accountant.
